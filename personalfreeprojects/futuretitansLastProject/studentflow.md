# ✅ **STUDENT JOURNEY DOCUMENT – Future Titans Innovation Challenge Platform**

## **Overview**

As a student, I enter a website built for the **Future Titans Innovation Challenge**.
This platform is not just a registration portal — it is a learning and mindset-building environment powered by AI and the **SURGE Framework™**.

The flow is:

1. **Register → Pay → Start Modules**
2. **Complete Module Lessons + AI Co-Founder Conversations**
3. **Get SSI Score (Entrepreneurial Mindset Score)**
4. **Unlock Final Submission Form**
5. **Fill the long innovation proposal form**
6. **Upload PDF + Video + Submit for the competition**

---

# 📌 **1. Registration & Onboarding**

### **Step 1: Visit Website → Register**

* I visit the platform homepage.
* I click **Register / Sign Up**.

### **Step 2: Fill Personal Details**

I enter:

* Full Name
* Email
* Phone
* Password
* Institution
* City & Country

### **Step 3: Payment**

I complete the required payment to participate in the challenge.

### **Step 4: Dashboard Unlocked**

Once registered, I’m taken to my **Student Dashboard**, similar to Coursera/EdX.

I see:

* **Modules Assigned**
* **Progress**
* **SSI Score (initially 0%)**
* **"Competition Form Locked"** message (until modules completed)

---

# 📌 **2. Modules Experience (Learning Program)**

The modules are structured like an online course.
Each module has chapters including:

* Video lessons
* Text explanations
* PDF resources
* Short assignments
* AI Interaction Chapters (most important)
* A forum for discussion

### 🎯 **AI = My Co-Founder**

In certain chapters, the AI activates:

* It *reads the chapter content* (because subtitles & text are provided to the AI).
* It remembers **all previous conversations** stored in the database.
* It understands my:

  * writing style
  * ideas
  * weaknesses
  * strengths
  * progress

### 💬 **AI Interaction Example**

The AI appears with a question:

> “Given what you learned about problem-solving, what is one issue in your school or city that bothers you the most?”

I answer.
The AI continues:

> “Interesting. Why do you think that matters? Who suffers the most from this problem?”

This goes on for 4–8 conversational rounds.

---

# 📌 **3. SURGE Framework™ Assessment During Modules**

Every AI chat evaluates me using **SURGE Framework:**

1. **S – Self Awareness** (understanding problems, introspection)
2. **U – Understanding Opportunities** (spotting ideas, identifying gaps)
3. **R – Resilience** (handling failure, responding to challenge prompts)
4. **G – Growth Execution** (thinking through solution execution)
5. **E – Entrepreneurial Leadership** (communication, initiative, clarity)

### 🧠 **How the AI Calculates SSI Score**

Based on my responses, Gemini API evaluates:

* Creativity
* Originality
* Decision-making
* Tone & confidence
* Problem clarity
* Resilience indicators
* Strength of reasoning
* Leadership signals
* Communication quality

After each interaction, SSI score updates.

The dashboard shows:

* **Awareness: 78%**
* **Exploration: 65%**
* **Resilience: 41%**
* **Growth: 52%**
* **Leadership: 27%**

---

# 📌 **4. Completion of All Modules**

Once I complete all modules and AI interactions:

A message shows:

> 🎉 “Congratulations! You are now eligible to submit your idea for the Future Titans Innovation Challenge.”

Now, the **Full Idea Submission Form unlocks**.

# 📌 **5. Competition Submission (Final Long Form)**

This is the form based on **Future Titans Innovation Challenge Phase 1: Idea Submission Template**.

It guides me to prepare a **PDF (max 5 pages)** with the following sections.

# 📤 **6. Upload PDF & Video**

Once the form is filled:

* I upload my **PDF (max 5 pages)**
* I upload or link my **video pitch**
* Click **Submit Entry**

The system checks:

✔ All sections completed
✔ Correct file name
✔ Correct PDF format
✔ Video uploaded
✔ Word limits followed

If all good → Submission successful.
